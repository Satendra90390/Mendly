## About / Bio

Mendly is a free, privacy-first health platform that helps you understand health topics, explore medicines, and find care — without replacing your doctor.

At the center of Mendly is **Elix**, an AI health companion that explains symptoms, medicines, and medical terms in plain language. Every response includes clear disclaimers: this is educational information, not a diagnosis.

### What Mendly includes:

🤖 **Elix AI Chat** — Ask health questions in plain language. Get educational explanations with source references.

💊 **Medicine Guide** — Search medicines by name. See uses, dosage, side effects, and precautions.

⚠️ **Drug Interactions** — Check if two medicines might interact. Get clear risk levels and next steps.

🏥 **Nearby Care** — Find hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies near you with live map + list view.

🚨 **Emergency** — One-tap access to emergency numbers worldwide, crisis resources, and first-aid guidance.

📊 **Health Dashboard** — Daily health tips, live health news, weather-based wellness suggestions, and vitals tracking.

🔒 **Privacy First** — Your health data stays yours. Delete it anytime from Settings. We never sell your data.

📱 **Works Everywhere** — Responsive design that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. No app download needed.

### Who is this for?

- Anyone who wants to understand health topics better
- People preparing for doctor visits
- Caregivers looking up medicines for family members
- Students learning about health and medicine
- Anyone who wants a calm, trustworthy health companion

### What Mendly is NOT:

- It is NOT a diagnostic tool
- It does NOT replace a healthcare professional
- It does NOT prescribe treatment
- It is NOT an emergency service

Mendly is built to inform, not to diagnose.
