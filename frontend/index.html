How It Works

Get started in seconds

Simple steps to access your medical companion.

Sign up in seconds with your email or Google account. No credit card required. Get 10 free AI chat messages to try everything out.
Chat with our AI assistant about symptoms, diseases, medicines, or treatments. Get detailed, accurate medical information instantly.
Browse our comprehensive medicine database with details on uses, dosage, side effects, precautions, and pregnancy safety. Filter by category or search by name.
Add medicines to check for potential interactions. Get severity ratings (Major/Moderate/Minor), clinical recommendations, and FDA citations.
Use location-based search to find hospitals and pharmacies near you. View ratings, contact info, and get directions with one click.
About Mendly

Making health information accessible for everyone

Mendly combines AI with comprehensive medical databases to help you make informed health decisions. Whether you need to check a medicine, find a nearby hospital, or ask about symptoms, Mendly is your 24/7 health companion.

Mendly provides informational guidance only and does not replace professional medical advice.
