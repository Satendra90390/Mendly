## Option A: Launch Announcement

**I built Mendly — a personal health platform that helps you understand health topics, not replace your doctor.**

After months of development, Mendly is live.

It's a free, privacy-first health companion that helps you:
- Chat with Elix, an AI that explains health topics in plain language
- Look up medicines, side effects, and drug interactions
- Find nearby hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies
- Access emergency contacts and first-aid guidance
- Track vitals, medications, and health data

**What makes it different:**
- Every response includes "not a diagnosis" disclaimers
- Medical sources and references are shown wherever available
- Your data is yours — delete it anytime from Settings
- Guests can try it free. No account required.

Built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JS + FastAPI. No heavy frameworks. No data selling.

Try it: [mendlyapp.web.app](https://mendlyapp.web.app)

## Option B: Story-Driven

**Most health apps want to replace your doctor. Mendly helps you talk to one.**

That's the core idea behind Mendly — a personal health platform I've been building.

The problem: people Google symptoms, get scared, and either panic or ignore it.

The solution: a calm, educational companion that:
→ Explains what symptoms might mean (without diagnosing)
→ Prepares questions for your next doctor visit
→ Shows medicine info with sources
- Helps you find nearby care when you need it

Every response from Elix (the AI companion) includes a clear disclaimer: "This is educational information, not a diagnosis."

Built vanilla. Shipped fast. Privacy by default.

Live at: [mendlyapp.web.app](https://mendlyapp.web.app)
